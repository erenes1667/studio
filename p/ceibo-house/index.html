05

Ten days, day by day.

Foundations first, then a review of your existing flows with sample versions to compare, then the two templates. Client review happens before anything goes live.

Days 1 to 5Days 6 to 10
  1. Day 1
    Access and audit

    Klaviyo, Shopify and DNS access. Run the 12-point audit and write down what is red.

  2. Day 2
    Fix the foundations

    DKIM, DMARC and the branded domain. Clean duplicates. Check form sync.

  3. Day 3
    Build the segments

    All six segments, checked against real profile counts.

  4. Day 4
    Feed and tracking

    Catalog feed, UTMs, Smart Sending and review sync set up and tested.

  5. Day 5
    Welcome flow, review and improve

    Review your current welcome flow. Compare it with the sample version here, and fix gaps. Build it only if missing.

  6. Day 6
    Abandoned checkout, review and improve

    Review the current flow and the order exit. Compare the cart-over-threshold branch with the sample. Build it only if missing.

  7. Day 7
    Post-purchase, review and improve

    Review the current flow and timing. Compare with the thank-you, review ask and cap cross-sell samples. Build it only if missing.

  8. Day 8
    Two templates

    Two email templates in the brand's look, checked at phone width.

  9. Day 9
    Client review

    Walk through the audit, the flow fixes and the templates together. Collect copy gaps: story, care steps.

  10. Day 10
    Sign-off and handover

    Client approves. Flow fixes go live one at a time, trigger checked first. Hand over the monthly campaign calendar.
