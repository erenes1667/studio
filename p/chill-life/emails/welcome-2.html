The tech-free break

Phone down. Look up.

A tech-free break does not need a plan. Put your phone in another room, or face down on the table, and give a few minutes to something that is not a screen.

  • Walk to the end of the street and back.
  • Make a drink and drink it slowly.
  • Call someone and let the call run long.
  • Sit and notice what is around you.

That is all. Pick your phone up again whenever you like. There is no streak to protect.
