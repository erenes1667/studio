# SAMPLE brand guide (written for this demo)

This file is a sample written by Enes Eren for this proof page. It is not Total Automotive's brand guide. It has no logo, no real brand colors and no client images. Replace it with the real guide on day one.

## Brand in one line
UK automotive claims management. Posts speak to drivers after an accident and to partner garages.

## Colors (neutral, sample)
- slate: #1F2A33 (backgrounds, headlines on light)
- stone: #EDEAE4 (light background, text on dark)
- graphite: #4A5560 (body text on light)
- mist: #B9C0C6 (rules, dividers)
- signal: #C8892B (accent, one use per design, CTA fill)

## Fonts (sample, must exist in the Canva account)
- Headline: Montserrat Bold
- Body: Open Sans Regular

## Tone
- Calm, plain English, second person ("you").
- Short sentences. No jargon. No legal threats.
- Never promise a result, an amount or a time. Never say "no win no fee" or "guaranteed".
- Never name a competitor or an insurer.
- Every driver-advice post ends with "General guidance, not legal advice." in the footer line.

## Assets (sample list)
- road-night-blur.jpg (abstract blurred road lights, neutral)
- clipboard-flat.svg (flat clipboard line icon)
- phone-camera-flat.svg (flat phone camera line icon)
- plain-texture-stone.png (flat stone texture)

## CTA wording allowed
- "Talk to us"
- "Check your claim"
- "Get a call back"

## Banned
- Emoji. Stock photos of crashed cars. Red warning triangles. Exclamation marks.
